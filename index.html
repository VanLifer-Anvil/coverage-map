Key

Coverage shown is each network's own predicted 4G and 5G outdoor and indoor coverage, combined into one map. It is a prediction, not measured signal. Hills, trees, buildings and vehicle bodywork can reduce signal, and actual speeds vary. Network maps dated December 2024 to March 2025. Covers Great Britain including the Hebrides, Orkney and Scilly; not Shetland or Northern Ireland.
