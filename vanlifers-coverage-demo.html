Indoor, outdoor — and inside your van

Good indoors

Predicted coverage suitable for use inside ordinary homes and buildings without an external aerial.

Outdoor coverage

Predicted coverage available outdoors, including areas where the signal may be too weak for reliable reception inside a building.

What about campervans and motorhomes?

These coverage predictions refer to ordinary buildings and outdoor conditions, not metal-bodied campervans and motorhomes.

A van's metal body can weaken mobile signals considerably. With a router connected to an external roof-mounted aerial or puck, the signal is received outside the vehicle, so outdoor coverage is the more useful comparison.

Remember: the indoor figures refer to conventional buildings. Don't assume they'll describe reception from a phone or router inside a metal-bodied van. If you use a roof-mounted aerial, look particularly at the outdoor figures.
